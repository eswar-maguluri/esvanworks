Common questions

What to know before getting started.

How long does a website take to build?

A typical website takes around 7–14 days after the required content arrives. The actual timeline depends on the agreed scope and how quickly feedback and approvals are provided.

Can you guarantee that my business will rank first on Google?

No. Search rankings depend on competition, location, your website and your business profile. Local SEO can improve the foundations, but specific rankings cannot be guaranteed.

How much does monthly website care cost?

Website care options are available for ongoing updates and maintenance. Pricing, included tasks and service limits are confirmed in writing before work begins. View Monthly Care details.

Can my website be in English and Telugu?

Yes. We can discuss an English website, a Telugu website or a bilingual website. Language, translation and content scope are agreed in writing before work begins. Explore language options.

What does the free website audit include?

The preliminary review covers mobile speed, Google Business Profile and Maps visibility, contact visibility and basic on-page SEO. It is not a formal audit and does not guarantee rankings, traffic or enquiries.
