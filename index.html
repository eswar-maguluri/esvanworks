Common questions

What to know before getting started.

How long does a website take to build?

A typical website takes 7–14 days after the required content arrives. The timeline depends on the agreed scope and how quickly feedback and approvals are provided.

Can you guarantee that my business will rank first on Google?

No. Search rankings depend on factors including competition, location, your website and your business profile. Local SEO can improve your foundations, but specific rankings cannot be guaranteed. Improvements often take 2–4 months or longer, depending on the starting point and competition.

How much does monthly website care cost?

Monthly website care starts at ₹1,999. The included tasks and any limits are confirmed in writing before work begins. View pricing details.

Can my website be in English and Telugu?

Yes. We can discuss an English website, a Telugu website or a bilingual website. The language, translation and content scope are agreed in writing before work begins. Explore language options.
