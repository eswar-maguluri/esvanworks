Content and compliance

Clinic content needs careful review.

Medical and healthcare communications may be subject to professional rules and applicable law. The clinic is responsible for reviewing and approving the information published on its website.

Approved claims and credentials

Use only accurate doctor credentials, services, qualifications and claims supplied or approved by the clinic. Do not publish unverified outcomes or misleading claims.

Patient testimonials and images

Do not add patient testimonials, endorsements, photographs or case details unless the clinic has confirmed they are appropriate and has obtained any required permissions.

Enquiry forms and privacy

Keep forms limited to the information needed for an enquiry. Avoid requesting medical histories or other sensitive health information through a general contact form.

If any form collects personal or health-related information, the clinic must review the privacy notice, data handling, access and retention arrangements before launch.

Appointments and emergencies

An enquiry form or WhatsApp link is not an emergency service and should not be presented as one. Any booking or patient management system requires a separate scope and review.
