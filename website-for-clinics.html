The problem

Patients should not have to search through several pages to contact your clinic.

Someone looking for a clinic may want to know which services you offer, where you are located, when you are open, and how to ask about an appointment. If those details are missing or difficult to find on a phone, the visitor may leave without contacting you.

A well-organized clinic website puts the important information in clear sections and gives visitors obvious ways to call or send an enquiry.

It also gives your clinic a place to share accurate information about its team, facilities, services, and contact details.

Designed for local patients

Useful on a phone, with English and Telugu options.

Many people check clinic details on their phones. The website should make the address, phone number, opening hours, and appointment contact options easy to use without excessive scrolling or zooming.

If your patients prefer Telugu, we can discuss an English and Telugu version of your website. Translated content and language switching are agreed as part of the project scope and price.

A clinic website can also link to your Google Business Profile and include accurate location information. These steps help visitors access your details, but do not guarantee a particular Google ranking or number of appointments.

Frequently asked questions

Clinic website questions.

Can patients enquire about appointments through WhatsApp?

Yes. The website can include a WhatsApp enquiry button and a click-to-call option. These make it easier to contact your clinic, but do not automatically create a booking system unless that feature is separately agreed.

Can you build the clinic website in Telugu and English?

Yes. A bilingual website can be discussed, including how visitors switch languages. The content, translation arrangements, and additional cost will be confirmed before work starts.

Can you help people find the clinic on Google?

I can help with website SEO setup and Google Business Profile setup. Local SEO can take around 2–4 months to show progress, and Google rankings or appointment numbers cannot be guaranteed.
