The everyday problem

Customers need the right information before they visit or call.

A potential customer might search for your business on a phone and want to check your services, prices where available, opening hours, address, or contact number. If the information is incomplete or difficult to find, they may move on to another option.

A business website gives you a central place to explain what you do and answer common questions. You can share that website on WhatsApp, social media, business cards, and your Google Business Profile.

It does not replace good service or guarantee new customers. It gives people a clearer way to learn about your business and decide whether to contact you.

Useful features

A website that works well on a phone.

Your website can include service or product pages, a menu or price list supplied by you, a gallery, opening hours, location details, customer reviews you are authorized to publish, and direct contact buttons.

Click-to-call and WhatsApp links make it easier for mobile visitors to contact your business. A map link can help people find your location, while a well-organized layout makes key information easier to scan.

If your customers use Telugu, we can discuss an English and Telugu version. Translation arrangements, language switching, and any extra cost will be agreed before work starts.

Local search setup

Make your business details consistent and easy to find.

For eligible businesses, Google Business Profile setup and website on-page SEO can help establish accurate business information online. Useful details include your business name, address or service area, phone number, hours, and services.

The work can also include page titles, descriptions, mobile usability improvements, and relevant links between your website and your business profile.

Local SEO may take around 2–4 months to show progress. Results depend on your business and competition. No specific Google ranking, traffic level, or number of enquiries is guaranteed.
