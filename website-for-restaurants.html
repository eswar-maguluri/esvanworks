Accurate business information

Keep your menu and restaurant details up to date.

Customers may use your website to decide what to order or when to visit. The restaurant should review its published information and notify us when agreed updates are required.

Menu items and prices

The restaurant is responsible for confirming item names, descriptions, prices, availability and dietary information. Any allergen information should be accurate and reviewed by the business.

Food photographs

Use genuine photographs that the restaurant owns or is authorised to publish. Images should not misrepresent the dishes being offered.

Opening hours and availability

Confirm normal hours, holiday schedules, temporary closures and any changes to service availability before publication.

Food and business claims

The restaurant should verify claims about ingredients, preparation methods, dietary suitability, certifications and other statements before they appear on the website.

Common questions

Questions about restaurant websites.

What can a restaurant website include?

A restaurant website can include a food menu, menu categories, business introduction, opening hours, location, contact information and links for customer enquiries. The final page list depends on the agreed project scope.

Can I display my food menu on the website?

Yes. Menu items can be organised into categories with descriptions and prices supplied and approved by the restaurant. The business is responsible for confirming that published menu information is accurate.

Can customers place orders or reserve tables online?

Online ordering, table reservations, payment processing and order management can be discussed as separate features. Their requirements, implementation and costs are confirmed before development.

Can the website be available in English and Telugu?

Yes. An English website, Telugu website or bilingual website can be discussed. Translation, content and page scope are agreed before work begins.

Will a restaurant website guarantee more orders?

No. A website can make menu and business information easier to access and provide contact options, but orders, enquiries, revenue and search rankings cannot be guaranteed.
