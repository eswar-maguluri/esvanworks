Property listings

Project or development pages

Present project information, available property types, location details and documents supplied by the owner or authorised representative.

RERA and property information

Use accurate, owner-approved property details.

Property descriptions, approvals and registration details should be supplied and checked by the responsible owner, agent or developer before publication.

RERA registration details

Where applicable, a listing or project page can display the relevant RERA registration information supplied by the responsible party. The number and related details must be verified before they are published.

Prices, availability and approvals

The property owner or authorised representative is responsible for confirming prices, availability, dimensions, approvals, ownership and other listing claims.

Photographs and documents

Use images and documents that you are authorised to publish. Do not present a sample listing as an available property.

Website development does not replace legal, title, regulatory or property due diligence.
