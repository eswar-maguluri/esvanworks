Common questions

Questions about salon and gym websites.

What can a salon or gym website include?

A salon or gym website can present services, facilities, opening hours, location information, staff or trainer profiles and customer enquiry options. The final page list depends on the agreed project scope.

Can customers enquire about appointments or memberships on WhatsApp?

Yes. Agreed WhatsApp and telephone links can help customers ask about salon appointments, gym memberships, classes and services.

Can the website be available in English and Telugu?

Yes. An English website, Telugu website or bilingual website can be discussed. Translation, content and page scope are agreed before work begins.

Can I add online appointment booking or membership management?

Online booking, membership management, payments and other advanced workflows require separate scope discussions. Requirements and implementation are confirmed before development.

Will a salon or gym website guarantee more customers?

No. A website can make business information easier to find and provide contact options, but customer numbers, enquiries, memberships and search rankings cannot be guaranteed.
