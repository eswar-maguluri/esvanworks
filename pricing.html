Common questions

Know what to expect.

How long does a website take?

A typical website takes 7–14 days after you provide the required content, images, and business details. Larger projects or delayed content can affect the timeline.

Will I own my website and domain?

Yes. You own your website and domain. Wherever possible, domain registration and third-party accounts should be set up in your name so you retain control.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds of revisions are included in the website project. New pages, new features, or changes beyond the agreed scope may cost extra. I’ll discuss these with you before proceeding.

How long does local SEO take to show progress?

Local SEO can take around 2–4 months to show progress. The timing depends on your business, competition, website, and Google’s systems. Rankings, leads, and specific results are not guaranteed.

How do payments work?

The standard payment split is 50% in advance and 50% on delivery. The final price, deliverables, and timeline will be confirmed in writing before work begins.

Can you add booking or order features?

Yes, booking and ordering features can be discussed. The cost depends on what the feature needs to do, so I’ll confirm the scope and price separately before starting.
