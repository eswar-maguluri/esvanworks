Common questions

Know what to expect.

How long does a website take?

A typical website takes 7–14 days after you provide the required content, images and business details. Larger projects or delayed content can affect the timeline.

Will I own my website and domain?

Website ownership and any transfer arrangements will be confirmed in writing. Wherever possible, domain registration and third-party accounts should be set up in your name so you retain control.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds of revisions are included in the website project. New pages, new features or changes beyond the agreed scope may cost extra. I’ll discuss these with you before proceeding.

How long does local SEO take to show progress?

Local SEO can take around 2–4 months to show progress. The timing depends on your business, competition, website and Google’s systems. Rankings, leads and specific results are not guaranteed.

How do payments work?

The standard payment split is 50% in advance and 50% on delivery. The final price, deliverables and timeline will be confirmed in writing before work begins.

Are GST and third-party costs included?

GST treatment and any applicable taxes must be confirmed before payment. Domain registration, hosting, renewals and other third-party fees are separate unless specifically included in the written scope.

Who pays for hosting, and can you migrate my site?

The hosting provider, account ownership, who pays recurring charges and any migration work will be agreed in writing before the project starts.

What does Monthly Care cover?

Monthly Care covers agreed routine updates, backup checks where supported, uptime monitoring, basic maintenance and security checks, and a monthly report. Specific limits and the handling of content changes will be agreed in writing.

Can you add booking or order features?

Booking, ordering, additional pages, Telugu versions and property listing features can be discussed. The scope and price will be confirmed separately before work begins.
