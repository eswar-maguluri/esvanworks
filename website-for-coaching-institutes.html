Content and accuracy

Present institute information clearly and responsibly.

Course descriptions, qualifications, results and other educational claims should be accurate and approved by the institute before they appear on the website.

Faculty credentials

Use qualifications, experience and professional details that the institute has verified and authorised for publication.

Course and fee information

The institute is responsible for confirming course descriptions, fees, schedules, eligibility requirements and admission conditions before publication.

Student results and testimonials

Do not publish unverified marks, rankings, selection claims, student photographs or testimonials. Obtain appropriate permission for any student information used on the website.

Enquiry forms and privacy

Keep enquiry forms limited to the information needed to respond to prospective students and parents. The institute should review its privacy and data-handling arrangements before launch.

Common questions

Questions about coaching institute websites.

What information can a coaching institute website include?

A coaching institute website can present course descriptions, faculty profiles, batch timings, location information, contact details and student enquiry options. The final page list depends on the agreed project scope.

Can students enquire about courses through WhatsApp?

Yes. Agreed WhatsApp and telephone links can help students contact the institute about courses and batches.

Can the website be available in Telugu and English?

Yes. An English website, Telugu website or bilingual website can be discussed. Translation, content and page scope are agreed before work begins.

Can I add online admission or student login features?

Admission workflows, student accounts, learning portals and payment features require separate scope discussions. Their suitability, requirements and implementation are confirmed before development.

Will a coaching institute website guarantee more admissions?

No. A website can make institute information easier to find and provide enquiry options, but admissions, enquiries and search rankings cannot be guaranteed.
