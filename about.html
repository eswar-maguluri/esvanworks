How projects work

A straightforward process, from discussion to delivery.

Each project starts by understanding the business, the intended audience and the work that needs to be done.

  1. Discuss. Identify your goals, audience, existing website and requirements.
  2. Define. Agree the scope, deliverables, price and expected timeline in writing.
  3. Build or improve. Work on the agreed pages, functionality and setup.
  4. Review. Check the work against the agreed scope and complete included revisions.
  5. Deliver. Confirm handover arrangements and any ongoing support requirements.

Actual timelines depend on project scope and when required content, images and business details are supplied.
