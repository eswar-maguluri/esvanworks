Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Esvan Works handles information when you visit this website or contact me about a project.

Last updated: 28 October 2026

1. Who operates this website?

Esvan Works is a software services studio operated by Eswar Maguluri in Guntur, Andhra Pradesh, India.

For privacy-related questions, contact contact@esvanworks.com.

2. Information you choose to provide

If you contact me about a website or other service, you may choose to provide information such as:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your business name
  • The service you are interested in
  • Details you include in your enquiry

Please do not send passwords, payment card details, identity documents, or other sensitive information through the website enquiry form.

3. How the contact form works

The contact form on this website prepares a message using the details you enter and opens WhatsApp so you can review and send it. The form does not itself submit your details to an Esvan Works website server.

Your information is sent only if you choose to send the message through WhatsApp. WhatsApp and its provider may process your information under their own terms and privacy policy.

4. How enquiry information is used

If you contact me, I may use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Discuss your project requirements and provide a quote
  • Coordinate website development or maintenance work
  • Follow up on a request you have made

I do not use information submitted through this website to create user accounts, and this website does not provide a customer login system.

5. Cookies, analytics, and technical information

This website is built as a static website. Its own HTML, CSS, and JavaScript do not require a user account or database to operate.

Hosting providers, including Cloudflare where applicable, may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, security events, and request logs as part of delivering and protecting the website.

If analytics or other third-party services are enabled on the website, those services may collect information according to their own policies. Check the deployed website configuration for the services currently in use.

6. Third-party websites

This website may link to third-party services, including WhatsApp, client websites, and email applications. When you follow an external link, that service's own privacy terms apply to your activity there.

Esvan Works does not control the privacy practices of those external services.

7. Sharing information

I do not sell personal information submitted through this website. Information may be processed by service providers involved in website hosting, communications, or the delivery of services you request.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, security, or the website from abuse.

8. Data retention and security

Enquiry messages may remain in the communication service you use and in my correspondence for as long as needed to respond to your request, manage a project, maintain business records, or meet legal obligations.

I take reasonable steps to handle project communications responsibly. However, no website or electronic communication method can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Your choices and requests

You can choose whether to submit an enquiry and what information to include. You can also contact me to ask about personal information associated with your enquiry or request correction or deletion where applicable law permits.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate business recordkeeping.

10. Children's privacy

This website is intended for people seeking business or professional services. It is not designed to collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when website features, services, or applicable requirements change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.
