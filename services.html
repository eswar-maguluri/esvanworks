Services FAQ

Questions about website and local SEO services.

What is included in a business website?

Page count, content, design, contact features and technical setup depend on the package and written project scope. The agreed deliverables are confirmed before work begins.

Can you help with Google Business Profile?

Profile setup or improvement can be discussed where the business is eligible and you can provide the required account access. Verification and Google’s decisions remain outside the developer’s control.

Can you build a Telugu website?

Yes. English, Telugu or bilingual page requirements can be scoped. Translation, proofreading and the number of pages should be agreed before work starts.

How do I choose the right service?

Start with the pages and customer actions your business needs. Compare the packages on the pricing page or contact Eswar to discuss the scope.

Can you set up a Google review QR code and short link?

Yes. A review collection setup can include a QR code and a short link to your Google review page. The scope and price will be confirmed in writing before work starts.

Can you add a Telugu version of my website?

Yes. A Telugu version can be scoped alongside your English website. The pages and content to be translated or prepared will be agreed in writing before work starts.

Can you add extra pages to my website?

Yes. Extra pages can be added beyond the original agreed scope. The page content, scope and price will be confirmed in writing before work starts.

Can you set up my domain and hosting in my name?

Yes. Domain registration and hosting accounts can be set up in your name. Domain and hosting fees are separate, and the setup scope and price will be confirmed in writing.
