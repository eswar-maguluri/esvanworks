02 / Get found

Local SEO for the searches that matter to your business.

Local search work helps make your business information clearer to search engines and potential customers. It does not guarantee a first-place result or a specific number of enquiries.

  • Google Business Profile setup or improvement
  • Page titles, descriptions and heading structure
  • Useful service and location information
  • Internal links between relevant pages
  • Google Search Console setup where included
  • Basic mobile performance checks

Local SEO improvements often take 2–4 months to become noticeable. Competition, location, listing quality and your starting point all affect progress.
